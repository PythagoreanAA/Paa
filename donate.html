Why it matters

A free reading, kept free.

Number & Soul exists to give people a clear, testable way to notice a pattern they already live inside — without a paywall standing between them and the first look.

Behind that free reading is ordinary work: writing, editing, fixing bugs, drawing the glyphs, answering questions, and slowly making the language clearer.

Your support pays for the time that goes into keeping this free, accurate, and growing.
