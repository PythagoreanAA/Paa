Method Before Myth

Operator first. Archetype second.

PAA does not begin by assigning colorful personalities to numbers. It begins with a transformation. Once the operation is clear, we can ask which recurring human and mythic figures most naturally embody that act. An archetype is therefore not proof of a number; it is a recognizable expression of an underlying operation.

I
Operation

What actually changes? Identify the before-state, the act performed, and the after-state.

II
Embodiment

Which recurring role, figure, image, vocation, or myth repeatedly performs that transformation?

III
Discrimination

Separate the healthy archetype from its distortion. The same operation can appear as gift, ordeal, excess, or shadow.

What Alignment Means

Recurrence, not costume

Archetypal alignment is strongest when the same operation recurs across different domains of a life: decisions, relationships, work, conflict, language, symbols, and turning points. Looking like an archetype is weaker evidence than repeatedly performing its transformation.

A Necessary Caution

Many masks, one motion

The Rebel and Explorer can both embody Five because both cross an established boundary. The Judge and Gatekeeper can both embody Four because both impose or preserve a boundary. PAA classifies beneath the costume, at the level of what the figure does.
